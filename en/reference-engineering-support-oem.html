Engineering support for an OEM

Joint development with a special machine builder.

Starting point

At a special machine builder, a drive flange failed after a few months of continuous operation. The machines were already installed at the end customer, so a fast and reliable solution was needed.

Challenge

Installation space was very limited, so the new part had to fit the existing machine. At the same time it had to last longer without costing more.

Our solution

  1. 1Failure analysis of the broken part and definition of the available space.
  2. 2Strength verification by FEA using the real load cases from operation.
  3. 3Design for manufacturing: fewer set-ups, standard tools, relieved notches.
  4. 4Prototypes, endurance test at the customer and release for series production.
