Technologies

Technologies & machinery

State-of-the-art manufacturing technology, a fully digital process chain and material expertise from aluminium to Inconel.

Competencies

Technology you can rely on

CNC machining technology

Modern controls (Heidenhain, Siemens), 5-axis simultaneous machining, high-speed cutting (HSC), minimum quantity lubrication (MQL) and precision workholding. Workpieces are measured in-process with touch probes and optics.

Digital CAD/CAM process chain

CAD modelling in SolidWorks and Inventor, CAM programming with HSMWorks/Fusion 360, machine simulation, central tool database, customised post-processors and DNC connection.

Metrology & quality assurance

Optical and tactile measuring systems, 3D CMM, inspection plans, certificates to EN 10204 and ISO-compliant inspection processes – including serial number management and traceability.

Resource-efficient production

Minimum quantity lubrication, sorted chip recycling and optimised tool paths noticeably reduce energy and material consumption.
