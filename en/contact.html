FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which file formats can I send?

Preferably STEP or IGES for 3D models and PDF for drawings. We also process native SolidWorks, Inventor and Parasolid data. Simply email your files to info@berben-engineering.de.

How quickly will I receive a quote?

Usually within 48 hours – for simple parts often on the same day.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

No. We manufacture from a batch size of one – from prototypes to small series of several thousand parts.

How fast can you deliver?

Prototypes from 24 hours with our express service, standard orders typically in 1–3 weeks – depending on complexity and quantity.

Do I receive inspection reports and certificates?

Yes. On request we supply inspection reports, first article inspection reports and material certificates to EN 10204 3.1.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship worldwide and can take care of customs and export documents on request.
