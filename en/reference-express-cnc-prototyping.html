Case study · Medical technology

Express CNC prototyping

High-end sample parts within 24 hours.

Starting point

A medical technology start-up had no presentable samples three days before a trade fair. The 3D data arrived on a Tuesday at noon.

Challenge

Functional samples in the later series material PEEK, not 3D prints. The parts were to be handled and moved at the fair.

Our solution

  1. 1Immediate feasibility check and feedback within two hours.
  2. 2CAM programming in parallel with material cutting.
  3. 3Overnight machining with cutting data adapted to PEEK.
  4. 4Measurement report of the functional dimensions and dispatch by courier the next day at noon.
