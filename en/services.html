Services

From first draft to inspected assembly

Six competencies, one continuous process chain: we develop, manufacture, assemble and inspect – so you can focus on your core business.

CNC machining CAD/CAM development System assembly Engineering & consulting Rapid Prototyping Quality assurance

CNC machining

High-precision 3- and 5-axis machining for single parts and series. Modern controls, thermally stable machines and precision workholding ensure micron-level accuracy – even on complex freeform surfaces.

  • 5-axis simultaneous milling and mill-turn machining
  • Tolerances down to ±0.005 mm, surfaces down to Ra 0.2
  • Workpieces up to 1,000 × 650 × 550 mm
  • Batch sizes from 1 to several thousand

CAD/CAM development

From idea to production: we design parts and assemblies in 3D, verify them by simulation and generate collision-checked NC programs – seamlessly integrated into your workflow.

  • 3D design in SolidWorks and Inventor
  • FEA simulation and tolerance analysis
  • CAM programming with machine simulation
  • Data import in all common formats (STEP, IGES, Parasolid)

System assembly

Complete assembly including functional testing, documentation and packaging – ESD-protected and under cleanroom conditions on request.

  • Mechanical and electromechanical assemblies
  • Assembly under cleanroom and ESD conditions
  • Functional and leak testing
  • Serial numbers, test reports and traceability

Engineering & consulting

Good parts are created at the design stage. Our engineers optimise your design early on for manufacturability, function and cost.

  • Design for manufacturing (DFM)
  • Material and process selection
  • Strength verification and part analysis
  • Cost reduction through process optimisation

Rapid Prototyping

Short lead times for first functional samples – from 3D model to machined part in a few days, or within 24 hours with our express service.

  • Express production from 24 hours
  • Prototypes in production material
  • Fast iterations with direct feedback
  • Seamless transition to series production

Quality assurance

For us, quality is not a final inspection step but part of every process. Parts are measured in-process on the machine and finally in our climate-controlled measuring room.

  • 3D coordinate measuring machine (CMM) and optical metrology
  • First article inspection reports (FAI) to VDA and PPAP
  • Inspection certificates to EN 10204 3.1
  • Statistical process control (SPC) for series
